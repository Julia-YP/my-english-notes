PART A · 01

课程信息与学习目标

课件 P1–2 · Contents

本课进入新单元 Chapter 2 Dining（用餐），主题是在餐厅点餐。三个目标：点餐词汇（4 主词）→ 餐厅功能句（May I take your order? 等）→ 讨论外出就餐与健康饮食。这是典型的「听说学练课」——课上要分角色朗读和大量开口，预习时把对话和问答答案读熟最划算。

预习提示 · 本课最重要的一件事预习重点
本课核心就一句：「服务员问 → 你答」的流水线：May I take your order? → Do you have any specials? → I'll take it.
— May I take your order?　— Yes. Do you have any specials tonight?　— Of course! We are offering lobster for only twenty dollars.　— OK! I'll take it!
这是课件 Dialog 页的骨架（Waiter + Emma），把这三句背到脱口而出，课上 Conversation 填空和角色扮演就全有底了。另外注意串课：offer 是 L11（Online Shopping）刚考过的真题词（We offer free delivery and free repair…），本课把它搬进餐厅——「提供」这个用法一课打通两个场景。
和已学课程的衔接扩充
· L11 Online Shopping（2026-10-10）：order / deal / original / discount——order 本课在餐厅语境「点餐」复现，一词两义。
· 同桌教材 A2-L15 餐厅预订餐桌 / A2-L16 餐厅点餐：已练过 a table for two / Are you ready to order?，本课是同场景的升级版（specials、free of charge 等新表达）。

PART B · 04

词汇精讲 · 4 张卡片

课件 Vocabulary 页 · 每卡 = 音标 → 双语释义 → 3 例句（含从句）→ 搭配 → 辨析 → 词形

按「课件模版」体例精讲：先读课件英文释义原文（课堂讲解素材），再过 3 个例句，最后看辨析防混淆。每张卡的例句都刻意带了从句，可直接当口语句子用。

第 1 卡 · free of charge 免费

WORD 1 / 4教材
free of charge
/ˌfriː əv ˈtʃɑːdʒ/ · 短语（形容词/副词性）
分文不取；免费。比 free 更正式，多放在 be 动词后作表语或动词后作状语。
课件释义 + 例句
without having to pay
e.g. These leaflets (传单) are offered free of charge.
英文释义 2 条教材
1. without having to pay（无需付款）
2. provided at no cost to the customer（免费提供给顾客）
3 例句（含从句，可直接口语用）预习重点
1These leaflets are offered free of charge, so you can take as many as you like. 这些传单免费发放，你想拿多少张都行。课件例句扩写
2The waiter told us that bread and butter is free of charge in this restaurant. 服务员告诉我们，这家店的面包黄油是免费的。
3You can park free of charge if you spend more than one hundred yuan in the restaurant. 在餐厅消费满 100 元就可以免费停车。
搭配与辨析扩充
free of charge = 正式、强调「分文不取」：be offered free of charge（本课课件搭配）
for free = 口语版：I got the bread for free.
free（adj.）= 免费的：The bread is free. / free parking——三种说法一回事，别混搭成 ✗ for free of charge。
词形家族 · charge扩充
charge n. 费用（service charge 服务费 · free of charge 免费）→ v. 收费（How much do you charge? 你们怎么收费？）→ in charge 负责（Who is in charge here?）——一个 charge 三场景，都是高频考点。

第 2 卡 · main course 主菜

WORD 2 / 4教材
main course
/ˌmeɪn ˈkɔːs/ · n. 名词短语
一顿分道上菜的正餐中最主要的那道菜。西餐三道式：appetizer 前菜 → main course 主菜 → dessert 甜品。
课件释义 + 例句
the largest or most important part of a meal in which there are different parts served separately
e.g. I had salmon (三文鱼) for my main course.
英文释义 2 条教材
1. the largest or most important part of a meal in which there are different parts served separately（课件原文）
2. the main dish of a meal, served after the appetizer（前菜之后上场的主体菜）
3 例句（含从句）预习重点
1I had salmon for my main course. 我主菜吃的是三文鱼。课件例句
2In most western countries, a full meal consists of an appetizer, a main course and a dessert. 在大多数西方国家，一顿正餐由前菜、主菜和甜品组成。
3What would you like to have for your main course, the steak or the roast chicken? 您主菜想要什么，牛排还是烤鸡？
辨析 · course vs dish vs meal扩充
course = 一「道」菜（上菜顺序中的一道）：a three-course meal 三道式
dish = 一「盘」具体的菜：My favorite dish is Kung Pao Chicken.
meal = 一「顿」饭：have a big meal——点餐时问 What would you like for your main course? 是最地道的问法。
词形家族 · course 一词多义扩充
course = 一道菜（main course）/ 课程（take an English course 修一门课）/ of course 当然——听力里靠语境区分。

第 3 卡 · special 特色菜（n.）

WORD 3 / 4教材
special
/ˈspeʃl/ · n. 特色菜；adj. 特别的
餐厅某天限时供应、平时菜单上没有的菜。点餐语境里 special 是可数名词，常以复数 specials 出现。
课件释义 + 例句
a dish that is available in the restaurant on a particular day that is not usually available
e.g. Today's specials are written on the board.
英文释义 2 条教材
1. a dish that is available in the restaurant on a particular day that is not usually available（课件原文）
2. a special price or deal offered for a limited time（限时特价）
3 例句（含从句）预习重点
1Today's specials are written on the board. 今日特色菜写在黑板上。课件例句
2Tonight we are offering a special: lobster for only twenty dollars. 今晚我们有一道特色菜：龙虾只要 20 美元。串 Dialog 页
3The waiter recommended the chef's special, which is not on the normal menu. 服务员推荐了主厨特色菜，普通菜单上没有这道菜。
辨析 · special vs specialty扩充
special（n.）= 限时特色/特价菜，天天可能换：Do you have any specials tonight?（本课功能句！）
specialty / speciality = 长期招牌菜、拿手菜：Peking duck is this restaurant's specialty.——一个是「限时」，一个是「招牌」，都和「特别」有关。
词形家族扩充
special adj. 特别的 → specially adv. 专门地 → especially adv. 尤其（L11 Further Study 短文原词！）→ specialist n. 专家。注意 specially = 专门（为某个目的），especially = 尤其（强调突出）。

第 4 卡 · offer 提供

WORD 4 / 4教材
offer
/ˈɒfə(r)/ · v. 提供，给；n. 提议；出价
主动提出给某人某物（对方可接受可拒绝）。双宾语结构：offer sb sth = offer sth to sb。
课件释义 + 例句
to provide or supply something
e.g. The hotel offers guests free use of the swimming pool.（截图低清，按图意重构，存疑）
英文释义 2 条教材
1. to provide or supply something（课件原文：提供、供给）
2. to say that you are willing to give someone something（主动愿意给予）
3 例句（含从句）预习重点
1The hotel offers guests free use of the swimming pool. 这家酒店为客人提供免费使用泳池的服务。课件例句（存疑重构）
2Tonight we are offering lobster for only twenty dollars. 今晚我们的龙虾只卖 20 美元。课件 Dialog 原句
3We offer free delivery and free repair if the product is still under warranty. 如果商品还在保修期内，我们提供免费送货和免费维修。L11 真题原句——offer 的串课复习
辨析 · offer vs provide vs give扩充
offer = 主动给（对方可以拒绝）：He offered me a drink, but I said no.
provide / supply = （客观）供应、提供：The restaurant provides free Wi-Fi.
give = 一般的给：give me the menu——点餐句里 offer sth for only $20（以某价提供）是本课重点搭配。
搭配与词形扩充
offer sb sth = offer sth to sb · offer a discount 提供折扣 · on offer 在售/打折中 · make an offer 出价（购物砍价语境，L11 bargain 家族）· 变形：offer–offered–offering（辅音双写 r）。

PART C · 06

Dialog 精读 · Emma 点龙虾

课件 Dialog 页 · 课堂将逐句朗读讲解

课件要求：请老师一句一句朗读并解释以下对话（原句低清，此条按版式重构，存疑）。场景是晚餐时间的餐厅。先自己读一遍气泡对话，再看下面的逐句讲解。

Waiter 服务员
Good evening. May I take your order?晚上好。请问可以点餐了吗？
Emma
Yes. Could we have some bread first? I want to know whether bread and butter is free of charge.好的。能先来点面包吗？我想问问面包黄油是不是免费的。截图低清，按对话逻辑重构，存疑
Waiter 服务员
Yes, bread and butter is free. Are you interested in a main course?是的，面包黄油免费。请问需要主菜吗？
Emma
Yes. Do you have any specials tonight?需要。今晚有什么特色菜吗？
Waiter 服务员
Of course! Tonight we are offering lobster (龙虾) for only twenty dollars.当然有！今晚我们的特色是龙虾，只要 20 美元。
Emma
OK! I'll take it!好！就要它了！
逐句讲解 · 3 个必抓语言点预习重点
1May I take your order? — may 比 can 更礼貌，服务员的标准开场白。order 在这里是名词「所点的餐」；take one's order = 帮客人记下点什么。回答直接说 Yes, I'd like the steak. 或先问 Do you have any specials?
2Are you interested in a main course? — be interested in + 名词/v-ing（对……感兴趣→想要……吗），比 Do you want… 委婉，是服务员推荐话术。
3I'll take it! — will 表即时决定（L11 学过：will 临时决定 vs be going to 计划）。点餐、买东西当场拍板都用 I'll take it / I'll have it。
朗读小贴士扩充
· May I 弱读连读：/meɪaɪ/；take your order 里 take your 连成 /teɪkjər/。
· Of course! 的语调上扬表热情；Tonight we are offering… 重音落在 lobster 和 twenty dollars。
· 角色扮演时服务员用升调问句，顾客答句降调收尾——老师大概率要求分角色读，提前进入状态。
PART C · 07

Conversation 填空预演 · 服务员 George

课件 Conversation 页 · 3 mins · 补全对话（答案在课件下一页）

课件玩法：假设你在餐厅，运用 Part A 学到的词汇表达补全对话。注意：George 是服务员（I'll be your waiter），不是顾客！课件 Hints 给了 5 个词条，正好对应 5 个空。本节序号与答案同时呈现——先自己试填，再对照。

对话填空（课件原文，2026-10-14 高清重读已校准）教材
AHi, I'm George. I'll be your waiter this evening. ①Are you ready to order? or do you need a few more minutes?
BI'm ready. I would like to have the mixed salad (综合沙拉).
AOk. What would you like to have for the ②main course?
BWell. ③Do you have any specials?
AYes. We are ④offering the roast chicken with vegetables tonight.
BI'll have it! And I think the soup is ⑤free of charge?
AYes, it's free.
＊课件 Hints：main course · offer · free of charge · Are you ready to order? · Do you have any specials?
＊课件下一页 Key：Are you ready to order / main course / Do you have any specials / offering / free of charge
逐空思路（与课件 Key 核对一致）预习重点
①Are you ready to order? — 后句 or do you need a few more minutes? 是二选一，前句必是「现在点单吗」；George 是服务员，站在服务员立场问。
②main course — 顾客已点 mixed salad（前菜），服务员接着问主菜；for the main course 固定问法。
③Do you have any specials? — 下一句以 Yes 开头，说明这里是问句；问特色菜正好引出烤鸡推荐。specials 用复数。
④offering — 空前是 We are，需 -ing 构成现在进行时，表「今晚特供」；＝主词 offer。
⑤free of charge — 答语 Yes, it's free. 反向确认；＝主词 free of charge。
填空预演策略扩充
先通读一遍判断每空说话人是谁：①②④是服务员（George）的台词，③⑤是顾客的台词。再抓三类信号词：菜名（mixed salad, roast chicken）、答句反推（Yes, it's free → 免费问句；Yes. We are… → 一般疑问句）、Hints 词条逐一归位——5 个词条与 5 个空一一对应，填完无剩余即对。
PART C · 08

Discussion 三问 · 双版本答案

课件 Discussion 页 · 3 mins × 3 问 · 惯例：版本 1 标准版（带从句）+ 版本 2 背诵版

三问的 Hints 全部来自课件原文。先自己答一版，再背版本 2 背诵版，最后合上书用关键词复述一遍。

Q1 · How often do you eat at restaurants? Why? Who do you usually go with when you eat at restaurants?教材
课件 Hints：once / twice / three times a week / month / year　·　seldom → love to eat at home / it's cheaper and healthier　·　with parents / friends / colleagues (同事)。旁注：eat out · nicer · enjoy the atmosphere · try new things · save time/energy。
版本 1 · 标准版
I eat at restaurants about twice a month, usually with my family or friends. We eat out when we want to celebrate something or when I am too busy to cook. Going to restaurants helps me enjoy the atmosphere and try new dishes, which I cannot make at home. But it costs more than eating at home, so I don't go every week.
版本 2 · 背诵版
About twice a month, usually with my family. It's a nice way to celebrate and save time, but it's more expensive than eating at home.
Q2 · Do you like to try new restaurants, or do you prefer to go to those you have already been to? Why?教材
课件 Hints：new ones → taste different dishes / have new experiences / learn about new cuisines and new cultures　·　always the same → already familiar with the environment and dishes / save myself time from thinking about what to order / avoid food I dislike。
版本 1 · 标准版（选「尝新」）
I prefer to try new restaurants, because I can taste different dishes and learn about new cuisines and cultures. Every new restaurant is a new experience — sometimes I discover a dish that becomes my favorite. Of course, familiar restaurants are safer because I already know the menu, but I think trying new things makes life more interesting.
版本 1 · 标准版（选「熟店」）
I prefer to go to restaurants that I have already been to. I am already familiar with the environment and the dishes, so I can save myself time from thinking about what to order. Besides, I can avoid food that I dislike, which makes eating out more relaxing for me.
版本 2 · 背诵版
I like trying new ones because I can taste different dishes and have new experiences. But when I just want a relaxing meal, I go to my usual place.
Q3 · Some experts say that most people have unhealthy eating habits. Do you agree or disagree? Do you know how to develop healthy eating habits?教材
课件 Hints：healthy eating habits → eat "real foods" such as vegetables, fruits and nuts / avoid artificial foods (人造食品) / keep a food journal / eat meals regularly (规律地) each day。
版本 1 · 标准版
I agree that many people have unhealthy eating habits. Some people often eat fast food and artificial foods because they are quick and cheap, and many of my friends skip breakfast or eat very late at night. To develop healthy eating habits, we should eat "real foods" such as vegetables, fruits and nuts, avoid artificial foods, and eat meals regularly each day. Keeping a food journal also helps, because we can see clearly what we ate and change our habits.
版本 2 · 背诵版
Yes, I agree. Many people eat too much fast food. To eat healthily, we should eat more vegetables, fruits and nuts, avoid artificial foods, and have meals at regular times every day.
答题套路拆解预习重点
三问共用一个结构：直接表态 → 给 1–2 个理由（用 because/which 从句撑长度）→ 可选补一句让步（Of course… / But…）。Q3 课件 Hints 里给的四招（real foods / avoid artificial foods / food journal / eat regularly）是现成的「理由库」，把它们嵌进 because 从句就是高分答案。
PART D · 09

实用功能句 · 点餐流水线

Review 页功能句 + Dialog/Conversation 提炼

按「进店 → 点单 → 问推荐 → 拍板」的顺序分组。Review 页明确要求掌握的两句是 May I take your order? 和 Do you have any specials?——这两句是本课的「锚句」，必须背到脱口而出。

① 开场 · 服务员问
1May I take your order?可以点餐了吗？（本课锚句）
2Are you ready to order?您准备好点餐了吗？
3What would you like to have for your main course?您的主菜想要什么？
② 顾客回应 · 问特色与免费项
1Do you have any specials tonight?今晚有特色菜吗？（本课锚句）
2Is the bread and butter free of charge?面包黄油是免费的吗？
3What's the most popular dish here?这儿最受欢迎的菜是什么？
③ 服务员推荐
1Of course! Tonight we are offering lobster for only twenty dollars.当然有！今晚龙虾特价只要 20 美元。
2I recommend the salmon. It is delicious with vegetables tonight.我推荐三文鱼，今晚配蔬菜很好吃。
3Are you interested in a dessert / appetizer?要来份甜品/前菜吗？
④ 拍板下单
1OK! I'll take it.好！就要它了。
2I'll have the roast chicken, please.我要烤鸡。（I'll have… 是点餐万能句）
3That sounds good. I'll go with your recommendation.听起来不错，就按你推荐的来。
PART D · 10

Further Study · 三道菜文化与课后音频

课件 See you next time + Further Study 页

课件结尾提示：「下一页有词汇扩展音频，记得收听！」Further Study 的 Listen and Learn 用三张图讲三道式西餐。这段是课后自学内容，也是下一课可能的抢答点。

三道式西餐一句话版预习重点
A full meal in most western countries has three courses: an appetizer to start, a main course (the biggest dish), and a dessert to finish.
背这句 = 同时复习 main course + 掌握 Further Study 的文化点。可以用在 Q3 答题里：In western countries, a full meal usually has three courses — an appetizer, a main course and a dessert, and sometimes the main course is too big, which is not very healthy.（把「量大」接进健康话题，一石二鸟。）
文化小贴士扩充
· bill vs check：英国说 bill，美国说 check——Could we have the bill/check, please?
· 面包黄油：欧美餐厅上桌先送 bread and butter，多数店免费，但有的高级餐厅会收费——本课 Dialog 就在考这个意识。
· 小费文化：美国餐厅一般给 15–20% 小费；中国和菲律宾不强制——和菲律宾外教聊这个话题很有共鸣。
PART D · 11

高频错误预测

按本课场景 + 往期课堂记录预判

根据 L11 课堂转写（时态误用、单复数、时态一致是重灾区）预判本课最容易被点名的错。先自查一遍。

Yes, I want eat steak. ✗ → Yes, I'd like / I'll have the steak, please.　点餐不用 want to eat，地道说法是 would like 或 will + 动词原形（I'll have = 我要）。
The bread is for free of charge. ✗ → The bread is free of charge. / I got it for free.　free of charge 和 for free 二选一，不能混搭。
Do you have any special tonight? （点菜语境） → Do you have any specials tonight?　special 指「特色菜」时是可数名词，any 后面用复数 specials（课件 Review 页原文也是复数）。
We offer to you a discount. ✗ → We offer you a discount. / We offer a discount to you.　offer 双宾语：offer sb sth，直接跟人；加 to 时 sth 要在 to 前面。
PART D · 12

仿写短文 · 本课词汇实战

目标表达已高亮

把 4 个主词 + 三道式织进一篇小短文（斜体为本课目标表达）。朗读到顺口，然后合上书、只看中文回译一遍。

Dinner at the New Restaurant
Last Friday my friends and I tried a new restaurant downtown. The waiter greeted us and asked, 'May I take your order?' Before ordering, I asked whether the bread and butter was free of charge — it was. Then I wanted to know if they had any specials. 'Of course! Tonight we are offering lobster for only twenty dollars,' the waiter said with a smile. For my main course I had the salmon, which came with fresh vegetables. After the meal we shared a chocolate cake for dessert. The whole dinner, from the appetizer to the dessert, was wonderful — and the free bread made it even better.
参考译文：上周五我和朋友试了市中心一家新餐厅。服务员招呼我们并问：「可以点餐了吗？」点单前，我先问面包黄油是不是免费的——是免费的。然后我问有没有特色菜。「当然有！今晚我们的龙虾特价只要 20 美元，」服务员笑着说。主菜我点了三文鱼，配新鲜蔬菜。饭后我们分了一份巧克力蛋糕当甜品。从开胃菜到甜品，整顿饭都很棒——免费面包更是加分。
一句话带走 · TAKEAWAY
Ordering in English is just three moves: 'May I take your order?' — 'Do you have any specials?' — 'I'll take it!'
英语点餐就三步：可以点了吗 → 有什么特色菜 → 就要它了。背熟这三句，今晚就能用。
